## 2. Corrections to docs/tricks.md

1. **§5 "Physics rework… T1 waits".** The rework merged as #45. Rebase the doc. §7 "Order" should say T1 can start, and that tuning waits for phase 2 items 1 and 2.
2. **§6.2 "gear preview, wake and tests that read BoardMesh".**
   - `AKiteSurfGearPreview` is a separate actor with its own `BoardMesh` (`KiteSurfGearPreview.cpp:48`). It is unaffected.
   - The wake reads `GetActorForwardVector` and only runs on the water (`BoardWakeComponent.cpp:140-149`).
   - No test reads the pawn's BoardMesh. What does need changing: `RigInput.Board` (`:1118`), the rider mesh location, and the board-level tests that drive landing with `SetActorRotation`.
3. **§6.2 split.** The root must stay a `UStaticMeshComponent` named `BoardMesh` with its collision, but hidden. `SafeMoveUpdatedComponent(..., bSweep=true)` and `SlideAlongSurface` (`:654-659`) depend on it being a primitive. Only `BoardVisual` is new.
4. **§6.1 pivot.** The trajectory point is the board centre, not the centre of mass. Rotating the body about the root would swing the rider around their feet. Add a visual centre-of-mass offset (§T1.2.6).
5. **§6.3 default roll "about 45° of inversion".** With an axis tilted β from Up, the body's Up reaches 2β from vertical. §6.6 counts an inversion below dot −0.3, which is a tilt over 107.5°, so β must be at least about 54°. With β = 45° the default roll never counts. Use `DefaultRollAxisTiltDeg = 65` (max tilt 130°, dot −0.64).
6. **§6.3 "stick towards the tail on screen is a back roll".** The chase camera looks along travel, so the tail is at the bottom of the screen. That direction is the Y axis, which the doc assigns to flips. Use instead: X towards the side of the screen the rider's back is on gives a back roll. The sign is latched at load start (§T1.4).
7. **§6.3 and T1.4 "new actions".** The left stick and WASD are already `IA_Edge` and `IA_WeightShift`. Mapping the same keys to a new `IA_Rotate` double-binds them (`bConsumeInput` and order effects).
   - Instead, reinterpret by state: during the load the stick is pre-wind; in the air it is rotation.
   - Conflict: during the load the stick also carves and shifts weight. Latch the board's carve and weight shift at their values when the load starts.
   - Gameplay change: no carving while loaded. It is configurable (`bPreWindLatchesBoardInput`).
8. **§6.1 line torque at its real scale stops off-line rolls.**
   - Hang tension of about 800 N on a 0.23 m lever gives about 180 N·m/rad. The potential barrier to go over is 2k, about 370 J.
   - A 2 s back roll carries about 50 J (I ≈ 10 kg·m², ω ≈ π rad/s).
   - So `LineTorqueScale` must be about 0.1 to 0.2, as the doc hints. Make it the T1.2 calibration knob, not a later tweak.
9. **§6.1 inertia.** I_up (about 2) is much smaller than I_⊥ (about 13), so the body is a symmetric top. Setting the pre-wind as L along a tilted axis gives a 500°/s twist. Set the pre-wind as **ω** (target rate on the axis, then L = I_w ω), and add capped off-axis "posture" damping (§T1.2.3). That damping also kills the line-pendulum ring and replaces the kinematic `RiderAirHangLeanDeg`.
10. **§6.1 "Step… after the board step".** Step the attitude **before** `StepBoard`. The board then sets the root yaw and runs the landing test with this step's attitude. Take-off is seen one step (4 ms) after a kite lift-off, and in the same step after `Jump()`, because the pop comes from input outside the loop.
11. **T1.2 `StepRateIndependentRotation (30, 60, 120 fps)`.** Under the fixed step this passes trivially. Also vary `SimStepSeconds` (1/120, 1/240, 1/480) on the bare component; that is the real integrator check.
12. **T1.2 `TuckSpinsFaster`.** T1 has no tuck input. Use the jump button held in the air as the tuck. This matches Shredders and the existing crouch.
13. **T1.5 vs existing behaviour.**
    - Today any yaw over 30° is a crash. With the table, 30 to 75° is Sketchy. That is a deliberate loosening; `KiteSurf.Jump.CrashRecovery` at 90° still crashes.
    - Do not change the signature of `OnBoardLanding(float)`. Add `OnBoardLandingVerdict(FLandingVerdict)`.
14. **T1.6 `RecognisesMegaloopBackRoll`.** It needs T0.3 loop records and T0.4 naming. It also needs phase 2 item 2 for a real megaloop: today the kite is low and to the side at the apex (CHANGELOG hang-time gap). Split it into a physics test (back roll on a ride) and a synthetic test (megaloop record plus roll gives the name).
15. **§6.1 hook lever.** `HarnessHookOffsetCm` (22, 0, 16) is measured from the pelvis (`KiteRiderPawn.cpp:159`). The attitude needs it relative to the centre of mass. Use `HookOffsetFromComCm` (12, 0, 20). Its equilibrium hang is atan(12/20) ≈ 31°, which matches today's `RiderAirHangLeanDeg` of 38°. Keep the two constants side by side and document them.
16. **Load at the pop.** `Jump()` zeroes `LoadAmount` (`:206`), so the attitude would never see it. Record `TakeoffLoad` and `TakeoffEdgeHold` in `BeginAirborne` and the pop path.

## 3. Conventions (one header: `Public/RiderAxes.h`, pure inline)

- **Body frame:** X = Front (chest), Y = Right = Up×Front (UE `CrossProduct(Up, Front)`), Z = Up (feet to head). This is the same frame as `FRotationMatrix::MakeFromXZ(Facing, Up)`.
- **Signs:** `FQuat(axis, +θ)` and `dv/dt = ω × v` agree with `FVector::CrossProduct`. Use those two everywhere.
- **Side** (towards the nose) = `-StanceSide · Right`. It is not needed for dynamics.
- **Take-off frame:** U = world up, T = horizontal velocity direction, S = U × T. σ = sign(R·T) at load start or take-off. If |R·T| < 0.2, fall back to the board's nose.
- **Back roll axis (world, at the start):** `A_back(β) = −σ·(cosβ·U_b − sinβ·F_b)`.
  - The spin part turns the chest towards −T (the tail) first: this is the doc's defining property.
  - The cartwheel part tilts the head towards the tail side, and at the half turn the head points −F (backwards).
  - Front roll = −A_back. Backflip axis = −R_b, front flip axis = +R_b (R × U = F, so rotating about +R tips the head forward).
  - The inversion sign κ (here −1 in front of sinβ) is `constexpr float RollInversionSign` and stays open until the reference clip (§9 open question). Tests assert only "chest to tail first" and the inversion count, so flipping κ does not break them.
- **BS/FS:** BS = spin sense −σ about U (same as a back roll), FS = +σ.

```cpp
namespace RiderAxes {
  inline FVector BackRollAxisBody(float Sigma, float TiltDeg) // body coords
  { const float B = FMath::DegreesToRadians(TiltDeg);
    return (-Sigma * FVector(RollInversionSign * FMath::Sin(B), 0.f, FMath::Cos(B))).GetSafeNormal(); }
  inline FVector BackFlipAxisBody() { return FVector(0, -1, 0); }

}
```

```cpp
FLandingVerdict LandingEvaluator::Evaluate(const FLandingInputs& In, const FLandingThresholds& T)
{
  auto Crash = [&](ELandingCause C){ return FLandingVerdict{ELandingGrade::Crash, C, 0.f}; };
  if (!In.bBoardAttached)  return Crash(ELandingCause::BoardOff);
  if (!In.bBarInHands)     return Crash(ELandingCause::BarLost);
  if (In.bPassInProgress)  return Crash(ELandingCause::PassUnfinished);
  if (In.BodyUpDot < T.InvertedBodyUpDot) return Crash(ELandingCause::Inverted);
  if (In.LandingG > T.CrashLandingG)      return Crash(ELandingCause::TooHard);
  if (In.TiltDeg > T.Sketchy.MaxTiltDeg)
    return Crash(In.ErrorAlongSpin >= 0 ? ELandingCause::UnderRotated : ELandingCause::OverRotated);
  if (In.YawOffVelocityDeg > T.Sketchy.MaxYawDeg) return Crash(ELandingCause::Sideways);
  const bool bHot = In.KiteElevationDeg < T.HotKiteElevationDeg;
  ELandingGrade G = ELandingGrade::Sketchy;
  if (In.TiltDeg <= T.Clean.MaxTiltDeg && In.YawOffVelocityDeg <= T.Clean.MaxYawDeg && !bHot) G = ELandingGrade::Clean;
  if (G == ELandingGrade::Clean && In.TiltDeg <= T.Stomped.MaxTiltDeg && In.YawOffVelocityDeg <= T.Stomped.MaxYawDeg
      && In.KiteElevationDeg >= T.StompedMinKiteElevationDeg && In.LandingG <= T.StompedMaxLandingG) G = ELandingGrade::Stomped;
  return { G, bHot && G == ELandingGrade::Sketchy ? ELandingCause::KiteTooLow : ELandingCause::None, T.SpeedRetention[(int)G] };
}
```

### T1.2.3 Maths, one fixed step (SI inside, one conversion at the boundary)

```cpp
void URiderAttitudeComponent::Step(float Dt, const FAttitudeInputs& In)
{
  PrevQ = Q; PrevComOffsetWorldCm = ComOffsetWorldCm;
  if (!In.bAirborne) {                       // slaved on the water / landing / crash
    Q = In.SlavedBodyQuat; StrapOffset = Q.Inverse() * In.SlavedBoardQuat;
    L = OmegaW = FVector::ZeroVector; bActive = false; bWasAirborne = false; return;
  }
  if (!bWasAirborne) BeginAir(In);           // take-off edge: §T1.2.4
  bWasAirborne = true; bActive = true;

  // pose -> inertia (critically damped tuck)
  SpringToward(TuckNow, TuckVel, In.Tuck, TuckSmoothSeconds, Dt);
  const FVector Ib = FMath::Lerp(InertiaStretchedKgM2, InertiaTuckedKgM2, TuckNow);   // body diag
  auto IWorldAbout = [&](const FVector& AxisW){ const FVector a = Q.UnrotateVector(AxisW);
       return Ib.X*a.X*a.X + Ib.Y*a.Y*a.Y + Ib.Z*a.Z*a.Z; };

  // 1) torques at state n (world, N*m)
  FVector Tau = FVector::ZeroVector;
  if (In.bLinesTaut) {                                   // r x F at the hook, about the CoM
    const FVector R_m = Q.RotateVector(HookOffsetFromComCm) / KiteUnits::CmPerM;
    const FVector F_N = In.LineForceUU / KiteUnits::UnrealForcePerN;
    Tau += LineTorqueScale * FVector::CrossProduct(R_m, F_N);
  }
  if (In.bRotationInput) Tau += ControlTorque(In, IWorldAbout);       // capped, toward target rate
  else                   Tau += AssistTorque(In, IWorldAbout);        // PD, only inside the window
  L += Tau * Dt;                                                       // kg m^2/s

  // 2) body-frame omega with implicit (exponential) damping: unconditionally stable, dt-consistent
  FVector Wb = Q.UnrotateVector(L) / Ib;
  const FVector A = CommittedAxisBody;                 // zero when nothing committed -> damp all
  const float Wa = A.IsZero() ? 0.f : (Wb | A);
  FVector Woff = Wb - Wa * A;
  const FVector WoffDamped = Woff * FMath::Exp(-PostureDampingPerS * Dt);
  FVector dLb = Ib * (WoffDamped - Woff);                               // posture torque * dt
  dLb = dLb.GetClampedToMaxSize(PostureMaxTorqueNm * Dt);
  Wb = (Q.UnrotateVector(L) + dLb) / Ib;
  Wb *= FMath::Exp(-AirAngularDragPerS * Dt);
  L = Q.RotateVector(Ib * Wb);
  OmegaW = Q.RotateVector(Wb);

  // 3) exact rotation for constant omega over the step; renormalise
  const float Ang = OmegaW.Size() * Dt;
  if (Ang > UE_SMALL_NUMBER) Q = (FQuat(OmegaW / OmegaW.Size(), Ang) * Q).GetNormalized();

  // 4) strapped board follows; take-off heel/pitch eases to the canonical strap offset
  StrapOffset = FQuat::Slerp(StrapOffset, CanonicalStrapOffset, 1.f - FMath::Exp(-Dt / StrapSettleSeconds));
  ComOffsetWorldCm = FMath::Lerp(ComOffsetWorldCm, FVector(0,0,ComAboveBoardCm), 1.f - FMath::Exp(-Dt / ComOffsetSettleSeconds));
  ensureAlways(!Q.ContainsNaN() && !L.ContainsNaN());
}
```

**Why this integrator**
- L is the state, so with no torque it is conserved exactly, and a tuck speeds the rotation up with no extra rule (ω = I⁻¹L).
- Gyroscopic effects come for free, because I_world rotates with Q.
- The rotation update is exact for the step's ω.
- Damping is an exponential decay, so it cannot overshoot at any step.

**Stability at 240 Hz**
- Line spring: worst case 6000 N × 0.23 m × 0.15 ≈ 210 N·m/rad on I_min 1.5, so ω_n ≈ 12 rad/s and ω_n·dt ≈ 0.05. Semi-implicit Euler is stable up to 2.
- Assist: ω_n = 7.5 rad/s.

**Frame-rate independence.** The step runs only at `SimStepSeconds`; the render uses Slerp(PrevQ, Q, α).

**Control torque.** `â_w = Q.RotateVector(AxisBody(stick, σ))`, using the mapping in §T1.4.
- `ω_t = |stick|·ω_full(family)`
- `τ_max = AirControlFractionPerS·I(â)·ω_full`
- `τ = clamp(I(â)·(ω_t − ω·â)/AirControlResponseSeconds, ±τ_max)·â`
- While held, `CommittedAxisBody = AxisBody(...)`, so posture damping does not fight the request.
- Released: no braking, so the rotation keeps its momentum.

**Assist torque.**
- **Active when:** `AssistStrength > 0`, no input, TTC < `AssistWindowSeconds`, and the error < `AssistMaxErrorDeg`.
- **Time to contact:** smallest positive root of `h + v_z t + ½ a_z t² = 0`, using the pawn's measured a_z filtered over 0.1 s; fallback `h / max(−v_z, 1 cm/s)`.
- **Target:** upright (body Up → water normal) with the board X along ±V̂₂D, whichever end is nearer; that gives two candidates, `Q_t = argmax |dot|`.
- **Error:** `q_e = Q_t·Q⁻¹`, made shortest (w ≥ 0), then axis e and angle θ.
- **PD:** `I_e = I(e)`, `Kp = I_e·ω_n²`, `Kd = 2ζ·I_e·ω_n`, `τ = Strength·clamp(Kp·θ·e − Kd·ω_perp, AssistMaxTorqueNm)`.
- While it acts, `CommittedAxisBody = 0`, so posture damping helps.
- It also exports `ErrorAlongSpin = dot(e, ω̂)` for the evaluator.

### T1.2.4 Take-off (`BeginAir`)

- `Q` is already the slaved kinematic pose (the lean included), so there is no visual pop.
- `StrapOffset = Q⁻¹·SlavedBoardQuat`.
- `CanonicalStrapOffset` = the board flat under the feet, X along ∓R per stance: `FQuat(FRotationMatrix::MakeFromXZ(-StanceSide*FVector::RightVector, FVector::UpVector))` in body coordinates.
- `ComOffsetWorldCm = Q.GetAxisZ()·ComAboveBoardCm`.
- **Pre-wind:**
  - `rate = PreWindAmount·|PreWindStick|·(PreWindLoadFloor + (1−PreWindLoadFloor)·TakeoffLoad)·ω_full(family)`
  - `ω₀_b = rate·AxisBody(PreWindStick, σ)`
  - `L = Q.RotateVector(Ib·ω₀_b)`
  - `CommittedAxisBody = AxisBody` (zero if there was no pre-wind).

### T1.2.5 Strapped board

`BoardQuat = Q·StrapOffset`. The board receives it every step (§T1.2.7). In T2, a board-off replaces it with a hand socket.

  - Re-arm when d > ArmDot.
- **Touchdown:**
  - `qRel = qLand·qTakeoff⁻¹`, then `SwingTwist(qRel, U)`: tilt = angle(swing), net heading = signed angle(twist) about U.
  - Whole turns come from `SpinRad`, not `qRel`.
  - Stance: Front₂D·towards-kite > 0 gives heelside. Otherwise: blind if the spin sense is BS (−σ), toeside if FS.
- **Component:** `UTrickTrackerComponent::StepTracker(float Dt)` is called in `StepSimulation` after `StepBoard`. It reads `Attitude`, `Board` and `Kite`, edge-detects take-off and landing, and fills `FTrickSignature`:
  - take-off stance, hooked = true;
  - inversions;
  - spin half turns and sense;
  - landing stance;
  - grade from `GetLastLandingVerdict()`.
  - Ticker parts on each credit: "Back roll", "Double back roll", "+ BS 180".
