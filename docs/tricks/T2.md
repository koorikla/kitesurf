## 0. Problems in `docs/tricks.md` when checked against the real code

1. **Heli loop rule (6.6, minimum elevation 55° or more) cannot happen.**
   - A loop only starts once the kite is 35° or more round the clock on the steered side (`KiteComponent.cpp:595`, `LoopClockDeg = 35` at `:107`). That is about 55° elevation at the window edge.
   - The turn radius is `MinTurnRadiusCm = 420` (`:75`) on 24 m lines, about 10° of arc. So a loop bottoms out about 20° below where it starts, at a minimum of roughly 35 to 45°.
   - Fix: use a relative rule. Heli = started after the apex, minimum elevation 40° or more, and the elevation drops by no more than 25°.
2. **S-loops cannot happen, and contra loops need a strange setup.**
   - Reversing the bar mid-loop clears `bLooping` (`KiteComponent.cpp:591`). A new loop then needs `Clock * SteerSide >= 35°`, which fails because the kite is still on the old side. The assist flies the kite across instead.
   - A contra loop therefore needs the kite parked 35° or more on the side the rider came from.
   - T2.4 has to change the kite's in-air loop entry (§T2.4 below). That is a kite physics change, so follow the `kitesurf-big-air-sim` skill.
3. **"Grabs are IK to sockets using the existing SolveTwoBone" (6.5) understates the rig work.**
   - Arm reach is 53.5 cm (`RiderRig.h:45-46`), the shoulders are 52 cm above the pelvis (`:50`), and the standing pelvis is 80 cm above the ankles (`:53`). A full crouch only takes it down to 48 cm (`:54`).
   - Worked through: even at a 30 cm pelvis height with the torso folded 60°, the toe-edge socket is about 68 cm from the shoulder.
   - Grabs need four things: a deep tuck, the torso folding at the hips (today the torso is rigidly along `BodyUp`, `RiderRig.cpp:48`), the board pulled up towards the body, and a reach test.
4. **The left stick has two jobs (6.3).** It is air rotation in T1.4 and also picks the grab zone "while held". Rule: while either grab button is held, the rotation torque input is 0 (momentum is kept), and the zone is latched (§T2.1).
5. **Bar hands are not specified.**
   - Real big air board-offs keep the front hand on the bar. Only a board pass takes both hands off.
   - The LB+RB chord must not mean "both hands off the bar".
   - The doc also never says how the kite steers with fewer hands on the bar (rule in §T2.1).
6. **Board mesh, sockets and straps.**
   - `generate_mesh_objs.py:430` says the board is 140 cm long, but `KiteGear.h:65` says `SM_KiteBoard` is modelled at 138. The comment and the C++ do not agree.
   - The board mesh has no straps and no handle. No sockets exist anywhere (grep finds none).
   - Only the gear preview scales the board length (`KiteSurfGearPreview.cpp:192`); the pawn's `BoardMesh` is not scaled.
7. **Fields missing for early or late roll.** It needs `PeakTensionTimeS` on `FKiteLoopRecord`, and inversion start and deepest-point times on the signature. Doc 6.6 lists neither.
8. **Loop record splitting is not stated.** The S-loop rule needs the tracker to split a loop record when the heading turn reverses. T0.3 says nothing about this.
9. **F5 and the judging rules disagree.** F5 says "a repeated trick does not raise the total"; GKA says "counts once" and takes the best. §T2.5 picks a rule.
10. **Test fixtures never run BeginPlay.** `FRideFixture` (`RideLoopTests.cpp:39-54`) spawns into a world that has not begun play, so `AKiteRiderPawn::BeginPlay` (`KiteRiderPawn.cpp:217-222`, where `OnBoardLanding` and `OnBoardCrash` are bound) does not fire. I believe UE defers BeginPlay until the world begins play; please confirm.
    - Trick code must be driven by explicit calls from the pawn's step, not by delegates bound in BeginPlay. Otherwise the ride tests silently pass on nothing.
    - The same applies to anything that subscribes to the session subsystem.
11. **"Natural" loop direction needs a sign in code.** It is pinned in §T2.4 from `KiteComponent.cpp:757` (`Right = Nose × Dir`, positive `TurnDeg` = turning to the rider's right) and `KiteSurfGameMode.cpp:90` (tack +1 rides to the right looking downwind).

### `RiderRig.cpp` changes

- **`:45-48`: split the hip frame from the torso.**
  - `HipFrame = MakeFromXZ(Facing, BodyUp)`.
  - `Pose.Torso = HipFrame * FQuat(Up, Twist) * FQuat(Side, Pitch)`.
  - The hips (`:75`, `:89`) use `HipFrame`. The shoulders (`SolveArms` `:110`) use `Pose.Torso`. The harness hook (`KiteRiderPawn.cpp:798`) keeps the torso frame.
- **`:66-83`: if `PelvisAnchor` is set, `Pose.Pelvis = *PelvisAnchor`** and the reach loop is skipped. Otherwise the height comes from `PelvisHeightCm(Crouch, Tuck)`.
- **`:85-93`, per leg:** the target is the strap when attached, otherwise `AnkleTarget`. The pole is `KneePole` if non-zero.
- **`:102-115`: `SolveArms` becomes two `SolveArm` calls with `DefaultElbowPole`.** The existing test stays valid.
- **Air contract (an amendment to T1's `StrapOffset`).** In the air the pawn sets `PelvisAnchor = Root + q·PelvisOffsetBody`, frozen at take-off. The strapped `BoardVisual` is `StrappedBoardFromPelvis(...)`. So a tuck pulls the board up towards the body instead of dropping the body onto the board. On the water nothing changes.
- **Grab reach fallback.** Add `RiderRig::SolveGrab(Pose, Side, SocketWorld, MaxBoardPullCm, OutBoardDelta)`.
  - If the socket is out of reach, it returns a board translation of at most 25 cm, plus a tweak roll about the board X axis of at most 20°, that brings the socket within reach. The pawn applies these to `BoardVisual` before the final solve; the feet follow because they are strapped.

**Zone mapping (stick sampled at peak magnitude during the first `ZoneSampleSeconds` after press):**

- Up = Nose, down = Tail. That matches W and S for weight on the nose or tail (`make_input_assets.py:99-101`).
- Left and right map to the toe or heel edge using `ChestOnScreenSign = sign(dot(RiderFront, CameraRight))`, read when the button is pressed. The stick pointing the way the chest faces on screen = toe edge.
- Inside the deadzone, `DefaultZone` applies.

### State machine sketch

```cpp
void TrickHands::Step(FTrickHandsState& S, const FTrickHandsInput& In, const FTrickHandsTuning& T, float Dt, FTrickHandsEvents& Ev)
{
  S.Clock += Dt;
  const bool bHeld[2] = { In.bFront && In.bAirborne, In.bBack && In.bAirborne };
  // Board-off: both held at once (from a grab, or both pressed within the chord window).
  const bool bChord = bHeld[0] && bHeld[1] && FMath::Min(S.SincePress[0], S.SincePress[1]) <= T.ChordWindowSeconds + Dt;
  switch (S.Board) {
  case EBoardPhase::Strapped:
    if (bHeld[0] && bHeld[1] && (bChord || S.Hand[1] == EHandPhase::Holding || S.Hand[0] == EHandPhase::Holding)) {
      S.Board = EBoardPhase::Removing; S.BoardT = 0.f; S.Variant = ResolveVariant(In.Stick, T.StickDeadzone);
      Ev.bBoardOffStarted = true; }
    break;
  case EBoardPhase::Removing: if ((S.BoardT += Dt) >= T.BoardRemoveSeconds) { S.Board = EBoardPhase::Off; S.BoardT = 0.f; } [[fallthrough]];
  case EBoardPhase::Off:
    S.BoardOffS += Dt;
    if (S.Variant == EBoardOffVariant::TicTac) S.TicTacDeg = FMath::Min(S.TicTacDeg + 360.f * Dt / T.TicTacSpinSeconds, 360.f);
    if (S.Variant == EBoardOffVariant::BoardPass) S.PassPhase = FMath::Min(S.PassPhase + Dt / T.BoardPassSeconds, 1.f);
    if (!(bHeld[0] && bHeld[1]) || !In.bAirborne) { S.Board = EBoardPhase::Catching; S.CatchS = 0.f; }   // let go: re-catch starts
    break;
  case EBoardPhase::Catching:
    if ((S.CatchS += Dt) >= T.RecatchSeconds) { S.Board = EBoardPhase::Strapped; Ev.bBoardCaught = true; S.TicTacDeg = S.PassPhase = 0.f; }
    break;
  }
  // Hands. During a board-off the back hand holds the board (both for BoardPass), the front stays on the bar.
  for (int32 H = 0; H < 2; ++H) {
    S.SincePress[H] = bHeld[H] ? S.SincePress[H] + Dt : 0.f;
    const bool bOnBoard = S.Board != EBoardPhase::Strapped ? (H == 1 || S.Variant == EBoardOffVariant::BoardPass) : bHeld[H];
    if (bHeld[H] && S.SincePress[H] <= T.ZoneSampleSeconds && In.Stick.Size() > S.StickPeak[H].Size()) S.StickPeak[H] = In.Stick;
    if (bOnBoard && S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::OnBar) { S.Hand[H] = EHandPhase::Reaching; S.HoldS[H] = 0.f; S.StickPeak[H] = In.Stick; }
    if (S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Reaching && S.SincePress[H] >= T.ZoneSampleSeconds && S.Zone[H] == EGrabZone::None)
      S.Zone[H] = ResolveGrabZone(S.StickPeak[H], In.ChestOnScreenSign, T.StickDeadzone, T.bThroughLegsGrabs);
    Spring(S.Weight[H], S.WeightRate[H], bOnBoard ? 1.f : 0.f, (bOnBoard ? T.ReachSeconds : T.ReturnSeconds) / 3.f, Dt);
    if (S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Reaching && S.Weight[H] >= 0.9f) { S.Hand[H] = EHandPhase::Holding; Ev.bHandArrived[H] = true; }
    if (S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Holding) S.HoldS[H] += Dt;
    if (!bOnBoard && (S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Holding || S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Reaching)) {
      if (S.Board == EBoardPhase::Strapped && S.Zone[H] != EGrabZone::None)
        Ev.GrabsEnded.Add({GrabName(ETrickHand(H), S.Zone[H]), S.Clock - S.SincePress[H], S.HoldS[H]});
      S.Hand[H] = EHandPhase::Returning; S.Zone[H] = EGrabZone::None; }
    if (S.Hand[H] == EHandPhase::Returning && S.Weight[H] <= 0.05f) S.Hand[H] = EHandPhase::OnBar;
  }
  // One-footer (T2.2): back foot only, strapped board only.
  const bool bFootOut = In.bOneFoot && In.bAirborne && S.Board == EBoardPhase::Strapped;
  const float Was = S.Foot;
  Spring(S.Foot, S.FootRate, bFootOut ? 1.f : 0.f, (bFootOut ? T.FootOutSeconds : T.FootReturnSeconds) / 3.f, Dt);
  if (S.Foot >= 0.9f) S.OneFootS += Dt;
  Ev.bFootOut = Was < 0.9f && S.Foot >= 0.9f; Ev.bFootIn = Was > 0.05f && S.Foot <= 0.05f;
}
EBoardAtTouchdown TrickHands::BoardAtTouchdown(const FTrickHandsState& S, const FTrickHandsTuning& T) {
  if (S.Board == EBoardPhase::Strapped) return EBoardAtTouchdown::Attached;
  if (S.Board == EBoardPhase::Catching && S.CatchS >= T.RecatchSeconds - T.RecatchGraceSeconds) return EBoardAtTouchdown::CaughtLate;
  return EBoardAtTouchdown::NotCaught; }
EFootAtTouchdown TrickHands::FootAtTouchdown(const FTrickHandsState& S) {
  return S.Foot <= 0.05f ? EFootAtTouchdown::In : S.Foot < 0.5f ? EFootAtTouchdown::Returning : EFootAtTouchdown::Out; }
```

### Classifier sketch

```cpp
float LoopClassifier::TackSign(const FVector& T, const FVector& Downwind, float StartClockDeg, float MinDot) {
  const float D = FVector::DotProduct(T.GetSafeNormal2D(), FVector::CrossProduct(FVector::UpVector, Downwind)); // +: riding right looking downwind
  return FMath::Abs(D) >= MinDot ? FMath::Sign(D) : (StartClockDeg >= 0.f ? 1.f : -1.f); // straight downwind: the kite's side is "front"
}
ELoopKind LoopClassifier::Kind(const FKiteLoopRecord& L, float W, const FLoopClassTuning& T) {
  if (FMath::Abs(L.TurnDeg) < T.MinCompleteDeg) return ELoopKind::None;
  if (L.StartFromApexS > 0.f && L.MinElevationDeg >= T.HeliMinElevationDeg && L.StartElevationDeg - L.MinElevationDeg <= T.HeliMaxDropDeg) return ELoopKind::Heli;
  if (L.RiderHeightAtStartM >= T.MegaMinRiderHeightM && L.MinElevationDeg <= T.MegaMaxMinElevationDeg && L.PeakTensionN >= T.MegaMinPeakTensionBW * W) return ELoopKind::Megaloop;
  return ELoopKind::Kiteloop; }
FLoopFamily LoopClassifier::Classify(TConstArrayView<FKiteLoopRecord> Loops, float Tack, TConstArrayView<FInversionRecord> Inv, float Airtime, float W, const FLoopClassTuning& T) {
  FLoopFamily F; int32 Main = INDEX_NONE;
  // Heli landing: the last completed heli loop, ending within the window before touchdown; not counted below.
  if (Loops.Num() && Kind(Loops.Last(), W, T) == ELoopKind::Heli && Airtime - (Loops.Last().StartS + Loops.Last().DurationS) <= T.HeliLandingWindowS) F.bHeliLanding = true;
  const int32 N = Loops.Num() - (F.bHeliLanding ? 1 : 0);
  // S-loop and snake: alternating halves of 150 deg or more, each starting within SGapS of the last one ending.
  for (int32 i = 0, Run = 1; i + 1 < N; ++i) {
    const auto& A = Loops[i]; const auto& B = Loops[i + 1];
    const bool bAlt = A.Direction == -B.Direction && FMath::Abs(A.TurnDeg) >= T.SHalfMinDeg && FMath::Abs(B.TurnDeg) >= T.SHalfMinDeg && B.StartS - (A.StartS + A.DurationS) <= T.SGapS;
    Run = bAlt ? Run + 1 : 1; F.bSLoop |= Run >= 2; F.bSnake |= Run >= 3; if (bAlt && Main == INDEX_NONE) Main = i; }
  // Count: the longest chain of completed kite or mega loops in one direction, gaps of ChainGapS or less. Base = the strongest kind in the chain.
  for (int32 i = 0; i < N; ++i) { const ELoopKind K = Kind(Loops[i], W, T); if (K == ELoopKind::Kiteloop || K == ELoopKind::Megaloop) {
      int32 C = 1; ELoopKind B = K;
      for (int32 j = i + 1; j < N && Loops[j].Direction == Loops[i].Direction && Kind(Loops[j], W, T) >= ELoopKind::Kiteloop
           && Loops[j].StartS - (Loops[j-1].StartS + Loops[j-1].DurationS) <= T.ChainGapS; ++j) { ++C; B = FMath::Max(B, Kind(Loops[j], W, T)); }
      if (C > F.Count) { F.Count = C; F.Base = B; if (Main == INDEX_NONE || !F.bSLoop) Main = i; } } }
  if (Main != INDEX_NONE) {
    F.bContra = Loops[Main].Direction * Tack < 0.f;   // natural = front hand = the kite turns towards the direction of travel
    const float Yank = Loops[Main].PeakTensionTimeS;
    for (const FInversionRecord& R : Inv) {           // the first inversion overlapping the main loop, or within 1 s after it
      if (R.StartS > Loops[Main].StartS + Loops[Main].DurationS + 1.f) break;
      if (R.DeepestS < Yank - T.RollMarginS) { F.Roll = ERollTiming::Early; break; }
      if (R.StartS > Yank + T.RollMarginS) { F.Roll = ERollTiming::Late; break; } } }
  return F; }
```

**Natural direction, concretely.** At take-off, T is the horizontal travel direction and `R_w = Up × Downwind` (UE cross order, as `KiteSurfGameMode.cpp:90` uses it). A loop is natural when `sign(Direction) == sign(dot(T, R_w))`. That is, riding right looking downwind, the right (front) hand pulls (Steer > 0), and positive `TurnDeg` means the kite turns towards the travel side. Otherwise it is a contra loop. If the travel is within about 75° of straight downwind, the side the kite started on stands in for the travel side.

Confirm this against one contra-loop clip before fixing the sign in tests (open question in tricks.md 9).

**Rules:**

- `Credited = Base × RepeatFactors[min(RepeatIndex, 4)]`. Crashes score 0 and do not increment repeats.
- `Total` = the sum of the top `Counting` credited scores, using the best credited score per distinct family key.
- At the horn, a jump in the air continues as `Overtime` and counts if it took off before the horn. Results come at its landing, or after at most 15 s.
- This satisfies F5's "an identical or similar repeat does not raise the total". A repeat scoring more than 1/0.75 of the original can still raise it, which matches GKA "counts once, the best version". Record that choice in `tricks.md` 6.8.
- Free ride uses the same ledger in `FreeRide` phase. Ledger and jumps reset on `StartSession`.
