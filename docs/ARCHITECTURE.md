# Architecture Specification

## Coordinate & Unit Conventions

Every unit constant and conversion is defined once, in `Source/KiteSurf/Public/KiteSurfUnits.h` (`namespace KiteUnits`): `CmPerM`, `CmPerKnot` (51.44), `UnrealForcePerN` (100), `GravityMS2` (9.81) and `GravityCmS2`, `AirDensityKgM3` (1.225), `WaterDensityKgM3` (1025), and `KnotsToCmS`, `CmSToKnots`, `KnotsToMS`, `MToCm`, `CmToM`, `NToUnrealForce`, `UnrealForceToN`. Code uses these instead of writing the numbers out.

- **Distance / Position**: Centimeters (Unreal units, cm).
- **Velocity**: Centimeters per second (cm/s) at the component APIs.
- **Forces**: kg*cm/s^2 between components (`UKiteComponent::GetLineForce`, `UBoardMovementComponent::AddExternalForce`; 1 N = 100); tensions and debug forces are in N. The kite's aerodynamics and the rider's air drag are done in SI (m, m/s, N) inside the step and converted once at the boundary.
- **Gravity**: `KiteUnits::GravityMS2` for the kite and the board alike, whatever the world settings say.
- **Wind Speed**: knots at the UI, cm/s in the simulation. Default `BaseWind` = 15 kn ≈ 772 cm/s at 10 m.
- **Water Plane**: World Z = 0.
- **Angles**: Degrees (-90° to +90° azimuth relative to wind direction).
- **Tunables** carry their unit in the name (`PlaningDragKgPerS`, `PopImpulseKgCmPerS`, `GravityTurnRadMPerS2`, `RiderDragAreaM2`, ...) or the tooltip.

## Tick Execution Order

The pawn owns the simulation (`AKiteRiderPawn::Tick`, `KiteRiderPawn.cpp`). The kite and board components do not tick on their own under it (`bCanEverTick` is false); the wind has no tick and is sampled where it is needed.

1. **Inputs**: the motion bar (if on and a controller with sensors is read) sets the steering and the bar; otherwise the held sheet input moves the bar; the mouse bar is read. The jump button held sets the board's load (`SetLoadHeld`); let go, it pops (`ReleaseLoadAndPop`) with the load built up so far.
2. **Restore**: if the root is still where the last frame drew it, it goes back to the simulation's own transform; if something outside the step loop moved it (a reset, a test), that is the new truth.
3. **Fixed steps**: the frame time, clamped to `MaxFrameSeconds`, is added to an accumulator, and `StepSimulation(SimStepSeconds)` runs while the accumulator holds a step, at most `MaxSimStepsPerFrame` times (a hitch drops the rest). Each step, in this order:
   1. **Kite** (`UKiteComponent::StepKite`): wind at the kite at the kite's simulation time; the rider's bar as it was one dead time ago; the assist; lift, drag, side force and weight; the line constraint and tension; the line force on the rider.
   2. **Line force** handed to the board (`AddExternalForce`).
   3. **Rider attitude** (`URiderAttitudeComponent::Step`, from `AKiteRiderPawn::StepRiderAttitude`): with the board's state at the start of the step, this step's line force and taut flag, the board's velocity, height above the water and filtered vertical acceleration, the pre-wind, the air rotation stick and the tuck. On the water it copies the riding pose (and the pre-wind builds while loading); on the take-off step it starts from that pose with the pre-wind's rotation; in the air it integrates the torques. The board gets its board, body and spin (`SetAirAttitude`) before it steps, so it turns and lands with this step's attitude.
   4. **Board** (`UBoardMovementComponent::StepBoard`): the line force added for the step is spent in it, whatever path the step takes (a crash's scripted stop included); the water under the board (five samples, the fitted plane, how fast it rises), the float depth from the speed and the pull (`FloatRiseTensionN`), the touchdown absorber starting or ending, the loaded crouch building or letting go (`LoadRatePerSec`, `LoadReleaseRatePerSec`; in the air too), lift-off (when the upward pull passes the rider's weight times 1 + `LoadHoldBonus` * load), the water's vertical force (the buoyancy spring, the planing lift, the absorber, the loaded hold), the heel (with the lean into a carve, the load and the lean back against the harness) and the sideways part of the heeled board's normal force, the hull's drag (with the pressure drag of the trim, and a floating rider's, `FloatingDragAreaM2`) against the velocity and the side force from leeway as exact solutions over the step, the rider's air drag in the air, the speed cap on the water only, orientation from the fitted plane and the heading on the water (the carve, the low-speed pivot, the switch of ends; with the lines taut the harness holds it within `MaxUpwindHeadingDeg` of the pull's beam reach and builds the lean back against the hook) and in the air the rider attitude's heading (without it the kinematic spin, auto-align and weight-shift pitch), move, the jump's height and distance and the ceiling at the clouds (`MaxJumpHeight`), landing (its load in g, hot, graded by `LandingEvaluator`, a crash only on a Crash grade; the jump count goes up).
   5. **Trick tracker** (`UTrickTrackerComponent::StepTracker`): reads the board's take-off, apex and landing getters (with the landing verdict's cause), the kite's loop records (the kite stepped its loop tracker at the end of its own step) and the rider attitude's body and angular velocity (stepped before the board, so on the touchdown step it still holds the body as it met the water), then steps the jump session, whose rotation recogniser counts inversions and spins; a finished jump gets its name, grade and score and goes to the trick book.
   6. With `kite.Physics.Debug 2`, a `kitecsv` line.
4. **Draw between steps**: the root and the kite's mesh are placed between the last two simulation states by the accumulator's remainder (`bInterpolateRendering`).
5. **Rider, camera, audio and debug**: the drawn board is turned and placed for the rider attitude in the air (between its last two steps). The jointed rider (`RiderRig`) is posed from the drawn board: feet in the straps, the body standing square across the board and leaning against the kite's pull on the water, lower in a crouch while loading (drawn letting go no faster than `LoadReleaseRatePerSec`, so the pop does not jump the pelvis), the attitude's body quaternion in the air (handed over from and back to the riding pose over `RiderHandoverSeconds`, torso and pelvis line together), hands on the bar; it tells the kite where the bar is. The camera looks along the heading, turned towards the kite far enough to keep it in frame (the clamp lets go as the kite nears the zenith, where its direction means little), tilts, widens and pulls back so the kite and the rider are always both in frame, and never rolls. On the water the heading is the board's yaw and the boom pivot rides on the board, tilting with it; in the air the heading is the horizontal velocity (the last heading is held under `CameraAirMinSpeedCmS`) and the pivot sits straight above the board, so spins, rolls and flips of the board do not swing the camera; the sound loops and the music follow the apparent wind, board speed, edge, line tension and the kite's airspeed; a hard yank on the lines buzzes the controller once; `kite.Physics.Debug 1` draws.
6. **HUD, wake and wind streaks** read the drawn transforms: the HUD's wind flag, wind window and speedometer, the jump readout and the trick card (polling the board's jump count and the tracker's record count), and the lesson layer (reading the lesson director's getters; the HUD draws after the world tick, so it shows the state the director reached this frame); `UBoardWakeComponent` (post-physics) lays foam and throws spray from the board's new position; `UWindStreakComponent` keeps its wind lines on the water round the rider.
7. **Best-three session** (`UTrickSessionSubsystem`, a tickable world subsystem, after the actors' ticks and not while paused): polls the tracker's record count, offers new records to the session, then ticks it by the board's simulation time with the tracker's live jump. Tests call `StepSession` after each pawn tick.
8. **Lesson director** (`ALessonDirector`, post-physics, after the pawn, only while a lesson runs): samples the board, kite and pawn getters into the lesson telemetry when a 60 Hz sample is due on the board's clock, then judges the lesson's current objective and moves its state machine. It never runs inside `StepSimulation`. Tests call `UpdateLesson` after each pawn tick.
