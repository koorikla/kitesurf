# Architecture Specification

## Coordinate & Unit Conventions

Every unit constant and conversion is defined once, in `Source/KiteSurf/Public/KiteSurfUnits.h` (`namespace KiteUnits`): `CmPerM`, `CmPerKnot` (51.44), `UnrealForcePerN` (100), `GravityMS2` (9.81) and `GravityCmS2`, `AirDensityKgM3` (1.225), `WaterDensityKgM3` (1025), and `KnotsToCmS`, `CmSToKnots`, `KnotsToMS`, `MToCm`, `CmToM`, `NToUnrealForce`, `UnrealForceToN`. Code uses these instead of writing the numbers out.

- **Distance / Position**: Centimeters (Unreal units, cm).
- **Velocity**: Centimeters per second (cm/s) at the component APIs.
- **Forces**: kg*cm/s^2 between components (`UKiteComponent::GetLineForce`, `UBoardMovementComponent::AddExternalForce`; 1 N = 100); tensions and debug forces are in N. The kite's aerodynamics and the rider's air drag are done in SI (m, m/s, N) inside the step and converted once at the boundary.
- **Gravity**: `KiteUnits::GravityMS2` for the kite and the board alike, whatever the world settings say.
- **Wind Speed**: knots at the UI, cm/s in the simulation. Default `BaseWind` = 15 kn ≈ 772 cm/s at 10 m.
- **Water Plane**: World Z = 0.
- **Angles**: Degrees (-90° to +90° azimuth relative to wind direction).
- **Tunables** carry their unit in the name (`PlaningDragKgPerS`, `PopImpulseKgCmPerS`, `GravityTurnRadMPerS2`, `RiderDragAreaM2`, ...) or the tooltip.

## Physics debug view: `kite.Physics.Debug`

A console variable registered in `KiteRiderPawn.cpp`, compiled out of Shipping. `UKiteComponent::bDrawDebug` forces level 1 for that kite's pawn.

- `kite.Physics.Debug 1` draws every frame. At the kite: true wind (blue), apparent wind (cyan), lift (green), drag (red), side force (magenta), line tension along the line (yellow, red when slack), heading (white), and airspeed, angle of attack, Cl, Cd, tension, taut or slack, clock and depth, and the fixed steps the frame ran. At the rider: the true wind at `RiderWindHeightCm` (blue), apparent wind (cyan), the line force on the harness (yellow), the board's side force from leeway (orange), the sideways part of the water's normal force on the heeled board (green) and its drag (red) from the last step, the board's normal tilted by its heel (purple, 1.5 m), the air's drag on the rider in the air (silver, at chest height), the five water samples under the board (white points on the surface, red while the touchdown absorber works) and the fitted plane's normal from the centre one (cyan, 1.2 m), and speed, heading, leeway, heel, the side and normal forces, board state, the board's height over the water, how fast the water rises there, the water's vertical force and the absorber, the last landing's g and HOT, and the gust factor. Beside the rider a bar shows the gust factor from 0.5 to 1.5. Arrows are 20 cm per m/s and 0.4 cm per N, so 10 m/s and 500 N are both 2 m long.
- `kite.Physics.Debug 2` also logs one line per fixed step to `LogKiteSurf`, starting with a header: `kitecsv,t_s,rider_x,rider_y,rider_z,rider_vx,rider_vy,rider_vz,kite_x,kite_y,kite_z,kite_vx,kite_vy,kite_vz,tension_n,alpha_deg,cl,board_state,gust,heel_deg,leeway_deg,side_n,normal_side_n,board_drag_n,water_z,surface_vz,water_up_n,absorbing` (cm, cm/s, N, deg; `board_state` is `EBoardState` as a number; `side_n` and `normal_side_n` are across the board, positive to its right; `water_z` is the fitted plane's height under the board, `surface_vz` how fast it rises there, `water_up_n` the water's vertical force on the board and `absorbing` 1 while the touchdown absorber works). `grep -o 'kitecsv,.*' Saved/Logs/KiteSurf.log | cut -d, -f2-` turns a ride into a CSV.

## Game Instance, Settings & Master Volume

- **GameInstance**: `UKiteSurfGameInstance` persists user settings across level transitions (`L_MainMenu` -> `L_OpenWater`).
- **Settings Persistence**: Game-specific gameplay settings (wind strength: 8–90 kn, kite size: recommended or 2–17 m², kite model, board size, rider, spot features, motion bar, vibration, master volume: 0.0–1.0) save and load via `UKiteSurfSaveGame`. Display and engine scalability settings (resolution, window mode, VSync, and overall quality scalability presets Low/Medium/High/Epic) are managed by `UGameUserSettings` (`Saved/Config/Linux/GameUserSettings.ini`) and mapped via `Config/DefaultScalability.ini`.
- **Trick book** (T2.7, fed by the trick tracker): `UKiteSurfSaveGame::TrickBook` (an `FTrickBook`, `Tricks/TrickBook.h`) lists every trick landed, keyed by `FJumpRecord::FamilyKey` and board category (twin-tip and foil kept apart; everything is twin-tip until a foil exists), with when it was first landed (UTC), times landed, and best score, height (m) and grade. Crashes never unlock or improve an entry. It lives in the same "Settings" slot: `UKiteSurfGameInstance` loads it in `LoadSettingsFromDisk` and writes it in `SaveSettingsToDisk` (through `ApplySaveGame` / `WriteToSaveGame`), and saves from before the book load with it empty. `RecordTrickLanding(const FJumpRecord&)` returns true on a first landing and does not write to disk; the pawn's `UTrickTrackerComponent` calls it after every finished jump, and the book reaches the disk with the next `SaveSettingsToDisk`.
- **Rider Character Materials**: Mannequin materials and textures (`MI_Manny_01_New`, `MI_Manny_02_New`, `M_Mannequin`, and physics asset `PA_Mannequin`) are committed directly under `Content/Characters/Mannequins/` from the Unreal Engine mannequin template assets to ensure `SKM_Manny_Simple` dependencies resolve cleanly without `LoadErrors`.
- **Master Volume**: Controlled via `FApp::SetVolumeMultiplier(MasterVolume)` both when settings are loaded from disk (`LoadSettingsFromDisk`) and when updated interactively (`SetMasterVolume`).
- **Menu videos**: `UKiteSurfMenuVideoSubsystem` (a game instance subsystem) plays `Content/Movies/Intro.webm` once when the main menu first opens (any key or click skips it; it ends on white, which fades off the menu) and then `Content/Movies/MenuLoop.webm` behind the main menu and the gear screen, through `KiteSurfMenuStyle::BuildBackdrop`. Playback is the Electra player with its VP9 decoder; `Config/DefaultGame.ini` stages `Content/Movies` as loose files. Without a renderer, without the files, or if the intro shows no frame within 4 s, the menus show the still `T_MenuBackground`. `PrepareLoadingScreen()` stops the loop and sets the movie player's loading screen before the ride level opens. The videos are filmed in the game by `scripts/render-menu-video.sh` with `AKiteSurfCinematicCamera` (`kitesurf.Shot`) and `kitesurf.CaptureFrames`.
- **Gear preview**: the gear screen spawns `AKiteSurfGearPreview`, a stand 1 km above the level. The chosen rider and board turn on it under the chosen kite. Its own `USceneCaptureComponent2D` draws only the stand (show-only list; every mesh is visible to scene captures only, so the game view never sees it) against a sky-and-sea card into a 720×900 render target, which the gear screen shows with ‹ › rider arrows and drag-to-turn. It is destroyed when the gear screen closes and keeps rendering over a paused ride. What each choice looks like is in one place, shared with the pawn and the kite: `RiderCharacter::GetStaticMeshPath` (null means the animated mannequin), `KiteGear::GetMeshPath` (`SM_Kite` 3-strut lime, `SM_KiteBoost` 5-strut orange) and `KiteGear::GetLengthScale`. A new rider or kite colourway is an enum value plus an entry there; `KiteSurf.Gear.EveryChoiceHasVisuals` fails until it has one.
- **Entry Level & Maps**: In standalone/packaged runs (`-game`), `GameDefaultMap` is `/Game/Maps/L_MainMenu` and uses `AKiteSurfMainMenuGameMode` configured via `L_MainMenu.umap` WorldSettings. `EditorStartupMap` remains `/Game/Maps/L_OpenWater`.

## Tick Execution Order

The pawn owns the simulation (`AKiteRiderPawn::Tick`, `KiteRiderPawn.cpp`). The kite and board components do not tick on their own under it (`bCanEverTick` is false); the wind has no tick and is sampled where it is needed.

1. **Inputs**: the motion bar (if on and a controller with sensors is read) sets the steering and the bar; otherwise the held sheet input moves the bar; the mouse bar is read. The jump button held sets the board's load (`SetLoadHeld`); let go, it pops (`ReleaseLoadAndPop`) with the load built up so far.
2. **Restore**: if the root is still where the last frame drew it, it goes back to the simulation's own transform; if something outside the step loop moved it (a reset, a test), that is the new truth.
3. **Fixed steps**: the frame time, clamped to `MaxFrameSeconds`, is added to an accumulator, and `StepSimulation(SimStepSeconds)` runs while the accumulator holds a step, at most `MaxSimStepsPerFrame` times (a hitch drops the rest). Each step, in this order:
   1. **Kite** (`UKiteComponent::StepKite`): wind at the kite at the kite's simulation time; the rider's bar as it was one dead time ago; the assist; lift, drag, side force and weight; the line constraint and tension; the line force on the rider.
   2. **Line force** handed to the board (`AddExternalForce`).
   3. **Board** (`UBoardMovementComponent::StepBoard`): the line force added for the step is spent in it, whatever path the step takes (a crash's scripted stop included); the water under the board (five samples, the fitted plane, how fast it rises), the float depth from the speed and the pull (`FloatRiseTensionN`), the touchdown absorber starting or ending, the loaded crouch building or letting go (`LoadRatePerSec`, `LoadReleaseRatePerSec`; in the air too), lift-off (when the upward pull passes the rider's weight times 1 + `LoadHoldBonus` * load), the water's vertical force (the buoyancy spring, the planing lift, the absorber, the loaded hold), the heel and the sideways part of the heeled board's normal force, the hull's drag (with the pressure drag of the trim, and a floating rider's, `FloatingDragAreaM2`) against the velocity and the side force from leeway as exact solutions over the step, the rider's air drag in the air, the speed cap on the water only, orientation from the fitted plane, move, the jump's height and distance and the ceiling at the clouds (`MaxJumpHeight`), landing (its load in g, hot, clean or a crash; the jump count goes up).
   4. **Trick tracker** (`UTrickTrackerComponent::StepTracker`): reads the board and kite after the board's step, steps the loop tracker from the kite's heading turn, then the jump session; a finished jump takes the board's landing sink and g (`GetLastLandingSinkMS`, `GetLastLandingG`), gets its name, grade and score and goes to the trick book.
   5. With `kite.Physics.Debug 2`, a `kitecsv` line.
4. **Draw between steps**: the root and the kite's mesh are placed between the last two simulation states by the accumulator's remainder (`bInterpolateRendering`).
5. **Rider, camera, audio and debug**: the jointed rider (`RiderRig`) is posed from the drawn transform: feet in the straps, the body standing square across the board and leaning against the kite's pull, lower in a crouch while loading, hands on the bar; it tells the kite where the bar is. The camera looks along the heading, turned towards the kite far enough to keep it in frame, and never rolls. On the water the heading is the board's yaw and the boom pivot rides on the board, tilting with it; in the air the heading is the horizontal velocity (the last heading is held under `CameraAirMinSpeedCmS`) and the pivot sits straight above the board, so spins, rolls and flips of the board do not swing the camera; the sound loops and the music follow the apparent wind, board speed, edge, line tension and the kite's airspeed; a hard yank on the lines buzzes the controller once; `kite.Physics.Debug 1` draws.
6. **HUD, wake and wind streaks** read the drawn transforms: the HUD's wind flag, wind window and speedometer, the jump readout and the trick card (polling the board's jump count and the tracker's record count); `UBoardWakeComponent` (post-physics) lays foam and throws spray from the board's new position; `UWindStreakComponent` keeps its wind lines on the water round the rider.
